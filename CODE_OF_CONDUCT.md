# Code of conduct

Tajdîn follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome people of every background and language, critique ideas and
not people, and assume good intent. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not
tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub
([@0xdolan](https://github.com/0xdolan)). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may
remove content or block participants who break these rules.
